What a good scoping review shows

  • A clear answer to “why is this review needed?” and “what questions does it answer?”
  • A map of what has been done so far, what is missing, and the gaps for future work.
  • A transparent method that meets PRISMA-ScR, including the items reviews often skip (Khan et al. among them): a registered protocol, a second screener, reasons for every exclusion, and a stated decision on critical appraisal.
Examples are paraphrased from Khan, Weidner, Rhee, Abdrabou, Bianchi, Velloso, Gellersen and Newn, “Gaze and Speech in Multimodal Human-Computer Interaction: A Scoping Review”, CHI ’26, doi:10.1145/3772318.3791662, licensed CC BY 4.0.
